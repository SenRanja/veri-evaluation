Saad Mish'al Al-Harthi, also transliterated Sa'ad Al-Harthi (Arabic: سعد مشعل الحارثي; born 3 February 1984) is a Saudi Arabian former football striker who played for Saudi Premiership side Al-Nassr FC. He retired from playing football in 2013.


== Career ==
On 14 March 2007, Al-Harthi was awarded the "Most promising Arab player" Award for 2006 given by the Lebanese Al-Hadth football magazine.
