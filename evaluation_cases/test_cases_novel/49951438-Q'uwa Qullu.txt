Q'uwa Qullu (Aymara q'uwa a medical plant, qullu mountain, "q'uwa mountain", also spelled Coa Kkollu) is a 4,425-metre-high (14,518 ft) mountain in the Bolivian Andes. It is located in the La Paz Department, Inquisivi Province, in the southern parts of the Colquiri Municipality. Q'uwa Qullu lies northwest of Ch'iyar Jaqhi.
