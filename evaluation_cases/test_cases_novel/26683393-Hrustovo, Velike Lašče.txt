Hrustovo (pronounced [ˈxɾuːstɔʋɔ]; German: Hrustou) is a small settlement west of Velike Lašče in central Slovenia. The entire Municipality of Velike Lašče is part of the traditional region of Lower Carniola and is now included in the Central Slovenia Statistical Region.
