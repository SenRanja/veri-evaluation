Selim Telib (Arabic: سليم طلب; born 14 March 2006) is a professional footballer who plays as a central midfielder for 2. Bundesliga club Hertha BSC. Born in Germany, he represents Egypt at youth level.


== Career ==
Telib is a youth product of Tennis Borussia Berlin, before moving to the youth academy of Hertha BSC in 2017. He started his senior career with the Hertha reserves in 2022. On 2 August 2023, he signed his first professional contract with Hertha until 2027.


== International career ==
Selim was born in Berlin to an Egyptian father and a German mother, The Egyptian-origin player had previously joined the junior and youth teams in Germany, but in the end he chose to represent the Pharaohs.
