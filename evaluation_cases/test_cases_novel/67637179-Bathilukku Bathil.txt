Bathilukku Bathil (transl. Tit for tat) is a 1972 Indian Tamil-language Western film directed by Jambu. The film stars A. V. M. Rajan, R. Muthuraman, C. R. Vijayakumari and Vennira Aadai Nirmala. It was released on 1 September 1972.


== Plot ==

A man seeks revenge on his friend, who betrayed him and stole his money, and harassed  his wife.
