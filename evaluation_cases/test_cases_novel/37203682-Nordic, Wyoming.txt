Nordic is a census-designated place (CDP) in Lincoln County, Wyoming, United States. The population was 602 at the 2010 census.


== Geography ==
Nordic is located at 43°4′45″N 110°59′28″W. It is located on U.S. Route 89, 7 miles (11 km) south of the town of Alpine.
According to the United States Census Bureau, the CDP has a total area of 5.1 square miles (13.1 km2), all land.


== Education ==
It is in the Lincoln County School District 2.
