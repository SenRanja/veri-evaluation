Krasnaya Dubrava (Russian: Красная Дубрава) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Rogozikhinsky Selsoviet, Pavlovsky District, Altai Krai, Russia. The population was 257 as of 2013. There are six streets.


== Geography ==
Krasnaya Dubrava is located 12 km southwest of Pavlovsk (the district's administrative centre) by road. Rogozikha is the nearest rural locality.
