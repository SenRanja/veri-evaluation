Michael Edwards (born 14 April 1974) is a former professional rugby league footballer who played in the 1990s and 2000s. He played at representative level for Wales and United States, and at club level for Oldham and Swinton, as a wing.


== International honours ==
Edwards won two caps for Wales while at Oldham in 1995.
