Nokha is a town and municipality in Bikaner District in the state of Rajasthan. 
Nokha was established as a Krishi Mandi by the contemporary king of Bikaner state Maharaja Ganga Singh. People of Nokha often compare Nokha to Chandigarh because it is a well planned city like Chandigarh. City is directly connected to Salasar Balaji temple and Tal Chhapar Sanctuary via State Highway-20. It is connected to all major cities of India by rail and road network. a famous shiva temple Sengal Dhora shiv temple is located around 30 km from this town.
