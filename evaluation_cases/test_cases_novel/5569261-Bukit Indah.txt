Bukit Indah (Jawi: بوكيت اينده) is a suburb in Iskandar Puteri, Johor Bahru District, Johor, Malaysia. The township has a population of over 60,000 with over 10,000 houses. As Western Johor's most populated town, its easy connectivity and its well-established shopping district has made Bukit Indah the center of the Western Johor commercial district.
The town prominently draws large crowds from Singapore as well as neighbouring townships such as Horizon Hills, Medini, Taman Sutera, Tampoi Utama, and Forest City.


== History ==
Bukit Indah was launched in 1997 by award-winning developer, S P Setia. Bukit Indah stands out as one of the few master-planned towns in Johor. Bukit Indah was built using an Australian "Green-Street" Concept emphasizing landscaped linear gardens. 
Bukit Indah's well planned design won the Johor State Landscape Award 2001, the National Landscape Competition 2001 and the Best of the Best National Landscape Award 2005. Bukit Indah's showpiece includes its 20-acre Central Park. Bukit Indah was the first town to introduce the town park concept in Johor Bahru.
On 24 May 2010, the Prime Ministers of Malaysia and Singapore have signed a landmark agreement to lower the toll charges for the Malaysia–Singapore Second Link significantly, thus benefiting the residents of Bukit Indah.


== Key Attractions ==
Bukit Indah stands out for its Shopping District which encompasses 1 major shopping mall, 2 hypermarkets and several F&B Zones. Bukit Indah has a sizeable Korean expat population, thus explaining the proliferation of Korean restaurants in the area.

== Transportation ==
Bukit Indah is a well-connected metropolitan town, with numerous public bus services connecting to Singapore, Johor and other parts of Malaysia.
Public Transport (from Singapore)
Travelers from Singapore are able to take Causeway Link Bus 3 (CW3) or Bus 6 (CW6) to reach Bukit Indah. Both buses start from Boon Lay, Singapore and would pass by key towns such as Medini and Horizon Hills before reaching Bukit Indah.
Alternatively, Singapore travelers can also reach Bukit Indah via the Skudai Terminal Tun Aminah (TUTA) located slightly north of Bukit Indah. The WTS 9 Bus provided by WTS Travel has the following pick-up locations:

Changi Airport
Tampines MRT Station
Punggol MRT Station
Woodlands CIQ
Johor Bahru CIQ
Skudai Terminal Tun Aminah (TUTA)
Public Transport (from other Johor Bahru areas)
Bukit Indah is also well connected to other major Johor Bahru hubs such as JB Sentral, Larkin or Puteri Harbour and Forest City. From JB Sentral Bus Terminal, Bukit Indah is accessible by buses 111 or 211 or J33, while Larkin can be reached by bus J44 and Forest City can be accessed via bus FC2. Public Transportation in Johor Bahru can be paid using cash, Mastercard/Visa or the Manjalink card. 


=== Road ===
In 2025, the new Nusajaya Coastal Highway and Interchange was inaugurated, greatly reducing the travel duration from the Causeway Checkpoint and the Second Link Checkpoint. From either checkpoints, Bukit Indah can be reached within 15 to 20 minutes via a private vehicle or taxi.
The Nusajaya Coastal Highway connects key towns such as:

Tuas Checkpoint (Singapore - Second Link CIQ)
Gelang Patah
Legoland Resort
Medini
Horizon Hills
Bukit Indah
Danga Bay
Johor CIQ
Woodlands Checkpoint (Singapore - Causeway CIQ)
In line with both governments to ensure faster immigration clearance, MACS (Malaysia Automated Clearance System) has been implemented. This facility allows fast-track immigration clearance for foreigners from 63 countries and territories including Singapore. This has greatly reduced passport processing time to 5 seconds (for QR scans) and 15 seconds (for passport scans) on average. 


==== Express Bus Terminal (to Singapore and other cities in Malaysia) ====
Alongside Larkin Bus Terminal, Skudai Terminal Tun Aminah (TUTA) serves as one of two main bus terminal hubs in Johor Bahru. Skudai Terminal Tun Aminah has express buses connecting Bukit Indah / Johor Bahru to other cities in Malaysia. Destinations include Malacca, Kuala Lumpur, Ipoh, Butterworth, Georgetown.  Skudai Terminal Tun Aminah (TUTA) also has an express bus connected to Changi Airport, Singapore.
