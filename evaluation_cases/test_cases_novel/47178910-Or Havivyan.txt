Or Havivyan (Hebrew: אור חביביאן; born 27 December 1994, in Zrahia) is an Israeli footballer who plays for Maccabi Ironi Netivot as a central midfielder.


== Career ==
He made his debut for Hapoel Be'er Sheva against Maccabi Sha'arayim in State Cup, in January 2014.
