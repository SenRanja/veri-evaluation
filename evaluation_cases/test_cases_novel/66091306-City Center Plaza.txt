The City Center Plaza (Chinese: 豐邑市政都心廣場; pinyin: Fēng yì shìzhèng dōuxīn guǎngchǎng) is a skyscraper office building located in Taichung's 7th Redevelopment Zone, Xitun District, Taichung, Taiwan. The height of the building is 168 m (551 ft), the floor area is 92,496.24 m2 (995,621.2 sq ft), and it comprises 38 floors above ground, as well as six basement levels. As of December 2020, it is the 5th tallest building in Taichung and 23rd tallest in Taiwan.
