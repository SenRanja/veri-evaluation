João Batista Bos Filho Airport (IATA: IJU, ICAO: SSIJ) is the airport serving Ijuí, Brazil.
It is operated by DAP.


== Airlines and destinations ==
No scheduled flights operate at this airport.


== Access ==
The airport is located 8 km (5 mi) from downtown Ijuí.
