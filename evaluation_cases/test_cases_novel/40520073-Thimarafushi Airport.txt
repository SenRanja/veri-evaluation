Thimarafushi Airport (IATA: TMF, ICAO: VRNT) is a domestic airport located on the island of Thimarafushi in Thaa Atoll, Maldives. It was constructed by the Maldives Transport and Contracting Company (MTCC); the first aeroplane to land at it did so in a test conducted on 2 September 2013. It was opened on 3 September 2013 by President Mohamed Waheed Hassan.
