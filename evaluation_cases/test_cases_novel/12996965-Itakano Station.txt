Itakano Station (井高野駅, Itakano-eki; station number: I11) is a train station on the Osaka Metro Imazatosuji Line in Higashiyodogawa-ku, Osaka, Japan. It is the northern terminus of the line and the northernmost subway station in the city of Osaka.


== Station layout ==
There is an island platform with two tracks underground. The platform is fenced with platform screen doors.
