Saint-Hilaire-Peyroux (French pronunciation: [sɛ̃t‿ilɛʁ pɛʁu]; Occitan: Sent Alari Peiros) is a commune in the Corrèze department in central France. Aubazine-Saint-Hilaire station has rail connections to Brive-la-Gaillarde, Ussel, Tulle, and Bordeaux.
