Aguni Airport (粟国空港, Aguni Kūkō) (IATA: AGJ, ICAO: RORA) is an airport serving Aguni, a village in the Shimajiri District of the Okinawa Prefecture of Japan. The prefecture operates the airport, which is classified as a third class airport.
