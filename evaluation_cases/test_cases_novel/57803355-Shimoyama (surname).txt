Shimoyama (written: 下山) is a Japanese surname. Notable people with the surname include:

Daichi Shimoyama (下山 大地; born 1989), Japanese basketball player
Hifumi Shimoyama (下山 一二三; 1930–2023), Japanese classical composer
Kenro Shimoyama (下山 研郎; born 1982), Japanese freestyle skier
Shinji Shimoyama (下山 真二; born 1975), Japanese baseball player
Takahiro Shimoyama (下山 貴裕; born 1992), Japanese basketball player
Yoshimitsu Shimoyama (下山 吉光; born 1976), Japanese voice actor
