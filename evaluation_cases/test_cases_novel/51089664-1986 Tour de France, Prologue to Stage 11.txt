The 1986 Tour de France was the 73rd edition of Tour de France, one of cycling's Grand Tours. The Tour began in Boulogne-Billancourt with a prologue individual time trial on 4 July and Stage 11 occurred on 14 July with a flat stage to Bordeaux. The race finished on the Champs-Élysées in Paris on 27 July.


== Prologue ==
4 July 1986 — Boulogne-Billancourt, 4.6 km (2.9 mi) (ITT)


== Stage 1 ==
5 July 1986 — Nanterre to Sceaux, 85 km (52.8 mi)


== Stage 2 ==
5 July 1986 — Meudon to Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines, 56 km (34.8 mi) (TTT)


== Stage 3 ==
6 July 1986 — Levallois-Perret to Liévin, 214 km (133 mi)


== Stage 4 ==
7 July 1986 — Liévin to Évreux, 243 km (151 mi)


== Stage 5 ==
8 July 1986 — Évreux to Villers-sur-Mer, 124 km (77 mi)


== Stage 6 ==
9 July 1986 — Villers-sur-Mer to Cherbourg, 200 km (120 mi)


== Stage 7 ==
10 July 1986 — Cherbourg to Saint-Hilaire-du-Harcouët, 201 km (125 mi)


== Stage 8 ==
11 July 1986 — Saint-Hilaire-du-Harcouët to Nantes, 204 km (127 mi)


== Stage 9 ==
12 July 1986 — Nantes to Nantes, 61.5 km (38.2 mi) (ITT)


== Stage 10 ==
13 July 1986 — Nantes to Futuroscope, 183 km (114 mi)


== Stage 11 ==
14 July 1986 — Poitiers to Bordeaux, 258.5 km (160.6 mi)
