Gradska Plaža Stadium (Macedonian: Cтадион „Градска Плажа“) or Stadiumi Plazhi i Qytetit is a multi-purpose stadium in Struga, North Macedonia. It has a capacity of about 800 and it is currently the home ground of FK Karaorman and FC Struga.
