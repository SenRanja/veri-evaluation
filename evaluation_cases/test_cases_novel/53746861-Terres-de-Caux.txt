Terres-de-Caux (French pronunciation: [tɛʁ də ko], lit. 'Lands of Caux') is a commune in the department of Seine-Maritime, northern France. The municipality was established on 1 January 2017 by merger of the former communes of Fauville-en-Caux (the seat), Auzouville-Auberbosc, Bennetot, Bermonville, Ricarville, Sainte-Marguerite-sur-Fauville and Saint-Pierre-Lavis.
