Lazany (Hungarian: Bajmóclazán) is a village and municipality in Prievidza District in the Trenčín Region of western Slovakia.


== History ==
In historical records the village was first mentioned in 1430.


== Geography ==
The municipality lies at an altitude of 313 metres (1,027 ft) and covers an area of 9.88 km2 (3.81 sq mi) (2025).


== Population ==

It has a population of 1840 people (31 December 2025).
