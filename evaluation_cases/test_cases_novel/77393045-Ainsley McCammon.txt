Ainsley Layna McCammon (born August 16, 2007) is an American professional soccer player who plays as a midfielder for Seattle Reign FC of the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL). She was signed by the Reign at the age of 16 in 2024. She captained the United States to third place at the 2024 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.


== Early life ==
McCammon grew up in Bedford, Texas. She played club soccer for Solar SC, leading the team to win the ECNL national championship in 2023 and being named an All-American in 2022 and 2023. At age 16, she signed an endorsement deal with Nike in April 2024.


== Club career ==
Seattle Reign FC announced on July 19, 2024, that they had signed McCammon to her first professional contract, under the Under-18 Entry Mechanism, on a four-and-a-half-year deal through 2028. She joined the team alongside Jordyn Bugg and became the youngest player in club history and seventh-youngest in league history at 16 years and 335 days old. Before going pro, she was committed to play college soccer for the Virginia Cavaliers.
The same day, McCammon made her professional debut as a 90th-minute substitute for Olivia Athens against the Utah Royals in the 2024 NWSL x Liga MX Femenil Summer Cup. Later that month, on July 31, she made her first professional start in a 1–0 loss to the Portland Thorns in the same competition. She made her NWSL debut as a stoppage-time substitute against Angel City FC on September 6. On October 5, she made her first NWSL start in a 1–0 loss against the same opponents, before going off to the 2024 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.
McCammon made her season debut with a start in a 1–0 loss to the Orlando Pride on April 12, 2025. On October 17, in the Reign's home finale, she scored her first professional goal, heading in a cross from Maddie Dahlien, to open the scoring in a playoff-clinching 2–1 win over the Utah Royals. She played in 16 league games, starting 7, and scored 1 goal in 2025, helping the Reign finish fifth in the league. In the playoffs, she started in the 2–0 loss to the Orlando Pride in the quarterfinals.


== International career ==
McCammon was called into training camp with the United States national under-15 team at the start of 2022. She won the 2022 CONCACAF Girls' U-15 Championship with the team. She was called up to the under-16 team later that year. She captained the under-17 team to win the 2024 CONCACAF Women's U-17 Championship. She remained captain at the 2024 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup, where she helped lead the United States to third place, its best result since 2008. She scored the team's second goal in a 3–0 win over England in the third-place match. She was called up by Emma Hayes into Futures Camp, practicing concurrently with the senior national team, in January 2025.
