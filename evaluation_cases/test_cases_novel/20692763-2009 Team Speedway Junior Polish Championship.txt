The 2009 Team Speedway Junior Polish Championship (Polish: Młodzieżowe Drużynowe Mistrzostwa Polski, MDMP) is the 2009 version of Team Speedway Junior Polish Championship organized by the Polish Motor Union (PZM). The defending Champion is Unia Leszno. The Final took place on 27 August 2009 at MotoArena Toruń in Toruń and was won by host team Unibax Toruń (41 points) where they beat Caelum Stal Gorzów Wlkp (38 points), Unia Leszno (33 points) and Marma Hadykówka Rzeszów (7 points).


== Qualifications ==
