Kety (Russian: Кеты) is a rural locality (a village) in Kultayevskoye Rural Settlement, Permsky District, Perm Krai, Russia. The population was 17 as of 2010. There are 3 streets.


== Geography ==
Kety is located 21 km southwest of Perm (the district's administrative centre) by road. Kichanovo is the nearest rural locality.
