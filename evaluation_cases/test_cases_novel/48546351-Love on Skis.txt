Love on Skis (German: Amor auf Ski) is a 1928 German silent comedy film directed by Rolf Randolf and starring Harry Liedtke, Rita Roberts and Georg Henrich. It was shot at the Bavaria Studios in Munich and on location in the Alps. The film's sets were designed by the art director Ludwig Reiber.
The film, in which Christa Tordy acted with her husband Harry Liedtke, was her final screen appearance.
