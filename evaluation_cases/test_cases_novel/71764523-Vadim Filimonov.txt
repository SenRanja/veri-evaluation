Vadim Donatovich Filimonov (Russian: Вади́м Дона́тович Филимо́нов; 14 January 1931 – 12 September 2022) was a Russian legal scholar and politician. A member of the Communist Party of the Russian Federation, he served in the State Duma from 1993 to 1999.
Filimonov died in Moscow on 12 September 2022, at the age of 91.
