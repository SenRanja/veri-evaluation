TCG Adatepe is the name of the following ships of the Turkish Navy:

TCG Adatepe (1931), one of two Adatepe-class destroyers in commission 1931–1954
TCG Adatepe (D 353), ex-USS Forrest Royal (DD-872), a Gearing-class destroyer acquired in 1971, scrapped in 1993
TCG Adatepe (F 251), ex-USS Fanning (FF-1076), a Knox-class frigate acquired in 1993, decommissioned in 2001
