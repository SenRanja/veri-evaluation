Who Did the Body is the second studio album by American rapper and songwriter Monaleo. It was released on October 17, 2025, through Stomp Down and Columbia Records. "Putting Ya Dine" and "Sexy Soulaan" were released as promotional singles on August 25, and September 26, 2025, respectively. The album was described as "southern gothic inspired" by the rapper.
