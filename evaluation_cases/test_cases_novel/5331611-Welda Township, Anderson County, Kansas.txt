Welda Township is a township in Anderson County, Kansas, United States. As of the 2010 census, its population was 290.


== Geography ==
Welda Township covers an area of 44.8 square miles (116.0 km2) and contains no incorporated settlements and one census-designated place, Welda. According to the USGS, it contains two cemeteries: Wardell and Welda.
