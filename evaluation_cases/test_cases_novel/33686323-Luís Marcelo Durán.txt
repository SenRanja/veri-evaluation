Luis Marcelo Durán (born August 31, 1974 in Montevideo, Uruguay), known as Marcelo Durán, is a former Uruguayan footballer who played for clubs of Uruguay, Chile and Peru.


== Teams ==
 Liverpool 1996
 El Tanque Sisley 1997–1998
 Racing 1999
 Huachipato 2000
 Central Español 2001–2003
 Melgar 2003
 Cerro 2004
 Fénix 2005
 Deportes Arica 2005
 Cerro Largo 2006–2007
 Fernández Vial 2007
 Cerro Largo 2008
 Central Español 2008–2009
 Juventud Las Piedras 2009–2010
