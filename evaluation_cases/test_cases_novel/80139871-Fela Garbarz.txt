Fela Garbarz (Felicja Garbarz, 1913–1943?) was a Polish actress of Jewish origin, member of the Jewish Actors' Trade Union in Poland (Polish: Związek Zawodowy Artystów Żydowskich w Polsce) She is known  for her roles in pre-war Jewish films and theater plays in Yiddish. During World War II she was in the Warsaw Ghetto, where she probably died.
