Plounéour-Brignogan-Plages (French pronunciation: [pluneuʁ bʁiɲogɑ̃ plaʒ]) is a commune in the department of Finistère, western France. The municipality was established on 1 January 2017 by merger of the former communes of Brignogan-Plages (the seat) and Plounéour-Trez.
