Pacy-sur-Eure (French pronunciation: [pasi syʁ œʁ]; lit. 'Pacy-on-Eure') is a commune in the Eure department, Normandy, France. On 1 January 2017, the former commune of Saint-Aquilin-de-Pacy was merged into Pacy-sur-Eure.
