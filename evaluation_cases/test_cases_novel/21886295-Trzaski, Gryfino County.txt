Trzaski [ˈtʂaski] is a settlement in the administrative district of Gmina Banie, within Gryfino County, West Pomeranian Voivodeship, in north-western Poland. It lies approximately 6 km (4 mi) north-west of Banie, 16 km (10 mi) south-east of Gryfino, and 31 km (19 mi) south of the regional capital Szczecin.
For the history of the region, see History of Pomerania.
