Auvers-Saint-Georges (French pronunciation: [ovɛʁ sɛ̃ ʒɔʁʒ] ) is a commune in the Essonne department in Île-de-France in northern France.
Inhabitants are known as Auversois.


== Geography ==
The village lies on the right bank of the Juine, which forms all of the commune's north-western border.
