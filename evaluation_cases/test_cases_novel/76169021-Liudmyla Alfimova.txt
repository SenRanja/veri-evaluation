Liudmyla Ivanivna Alfimova (Ukrainian: Людмила Іванівна Алфімова; 4 September 1935 – 22 February 2024) was a Ukrainian actress. She graduated from the Kharkiv Theatre Institute in 1958 and began working for Dovzhenko Film Studios.
