Floris van der Linden (born 10 April 1996) is a Dutch footballer who plays as a forward for Tweede Divisie club Spakenburg.


== Club career ==
Van der Linden made his professional debut in the Eerste Divisie for SC Telstar on 8 August 2016 in a game against RKC Waalwijk.
