Mind Your Back, Professor (Danish: Pas på ryggen, professor) is a 1977 Danish comedy film directed by Jens Okking and starring Ulf Pilgaard. The film is actor Jens Okking's debut as a director and screenwriter and is loosely based on Orla Johansen's novel Ryggen, professor!
