Robert "Bob" Kelly (born 3 May 1955) is a Scottish curler and curling coach from Edinburgh.
He is a 1995 World Men's silver medallist and three-time Scottish men's champion.  He also participated as a member of Great Britain men's team at the 1992 Winter Olympics (where curling was a demonstration sport); the team finished in 5th place.
