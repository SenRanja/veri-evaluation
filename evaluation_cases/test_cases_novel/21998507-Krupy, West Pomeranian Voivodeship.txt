Krupy [ˈkrupɨ] (German: Grupenhagen) is a village in the administrative district of Gmina Darłowo, within Sławno County, West Pomeranian Voivodeship, in north-western Poland. It lies approximately 7 km (4 mi) east of Darłowo, 13 km (8 mi) north-west of Sławno, and 169 km (105 mi) north-east of the regional capital Szczecin.
The village has a population of 361.
For the history of the region, see History of Pomerania.
