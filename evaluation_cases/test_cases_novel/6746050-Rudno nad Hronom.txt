Rudno nad Hronom (Hungarian: Garamrudnó) is a village and municipality in the Žarnovica District, Banská Bystrica Region in Slovakia.


== Geography ==
The municipality lies at an altitude of 207 metres (679 ft) and covers an area of 19.28 km2 (7.44 sq mi) (2025).


== Population ==

It has a population of 517 people (31 December 2025).
