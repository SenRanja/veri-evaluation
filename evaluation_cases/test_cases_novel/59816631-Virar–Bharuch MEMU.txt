The 19101/19102 Virar - Bharuch MEMU is a MEMU express train of the Indian Railways connecting Virar in Maharashtra and Bharuch Junction of Gujarat. It is currently being operated with 19101/19102 train numbers on a daily basis.


== Service ==
The 19101/Virar–Bharuch MEMU has average speed of 40 km/h and covers 267 km in 6 hrs 45 mins.
The 19102/Bharuch–Virar MEMU has average speed of 40 km/h and covers 267 km in 7 hrs 15 mins.


== Route ==
The 19101/02 Virar–Bharuch MEMU runs from Virar via Palghar, Dahanu Road, Vapi, Valsad, Navsari, Surat, Kim, Kosamba Junction, Ankleshwar Junction to Bharuch Junction and vice versa.


== Coach composite ==
The train consists of 20 MEMU Rake Coaches.
