Thijs Aerts (born 3 November 1996) is a Belgian cyclist, who currently rides for UCI Continental team Wanty–Nippo–ReUz. He competed in the men's under-23 event at the 2016 UCI Cyclo-cross World Championships in Heusden-Zolder. His brother Toon is also a professional cyclist.
