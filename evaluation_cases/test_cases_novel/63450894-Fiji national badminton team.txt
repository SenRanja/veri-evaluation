The Fiji national badminton team (Fijian: Timi ni dautaqani ni matanitu o Viti) represents Fiji in international badminton team competitions. It is administered by the Fiji Badminton Association. Fiji competed in the 2017 Sudirman Cup,  where it finished in 25th place.
The Fijian mixed team won bronze three times in the Oceania Badminton Championships. The women's team also won bronze in the 2018 Oceania Badminton Championships.
