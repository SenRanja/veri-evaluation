Sarlak (Russian: Сарлак; Bashkir: Сарлаҡ, Sarlaq) is a rural locality (a village) in Meleuzovsky Selsoviet, Meleuzovsky District, Bashkortostan, Russia. The population was 334 as of 2010. There are 6 streets.


== Geography ==
Sarlak is located 7 km north of Meleuz (the district's administrative centre) by road. Meleuz is the nearest rural locality.
