Voronovo (Russian: Вороново) is a rural locality (a village) in Ustyuzhenskoye Rural Settlement, Ustyuzhensky District, Vologda Oblast, Russia. The population was 17 as of 2002.


== Geography ==
Voronovo is located 14 km (8.7 mi) south of Ustyuzhna (the district's administrative centre) by road. Timonino is the nearest rural locality.
