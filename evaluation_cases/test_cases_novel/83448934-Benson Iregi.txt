Benson Iregi (born 7 October 1989) is a Kenyan former footballer who played as a winger for Kenyan Premier League clubs including Nairobi City Stars, Thika United, and Bidco United.


== Club career ==
Iregi began his football career at Kawangware-based Intercity FC from where he joined Nairobi City Stars for the 2016 season albeit on loan. Following his spell at City Stars, Iregi moved to Thika United at the onset of the 2017 season. Years later he returned to the top flight to feature for Bidco United.


== International career ==
Iregi represented Kenya All-Stars squad that toured Spain in 2017, an initiative aimed at exposing Kenyan footballers to European football and providing international experience for emerging talents.
