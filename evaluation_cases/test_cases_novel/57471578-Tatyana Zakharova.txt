Tatyana Zakharova (Russian: Татьяна Захарова; born April 18, 1969) was a professional sprinter from Russia. She won a silver medal in the 4 × 400 m relay at the 1995 World Championships in Athletics by virtue of running for her team in the preliminary rounds.
She also won a silver medal in the 4 × 400 m at the 1994 Goodwill Games, this time running in the final.
