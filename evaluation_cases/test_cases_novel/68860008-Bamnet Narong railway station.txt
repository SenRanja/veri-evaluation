Bamnet Narong station (Thai: สถานีบำเหน็จณรงค์) is a railway station located in Ban Phet Subdistrict, Bamnet Narong District, Chaiyaphum Province. It is a class 2 railway station located 290.53 km (180.5 mi) from Bangkok railway station and is the main station for Bamnet Narong District.
