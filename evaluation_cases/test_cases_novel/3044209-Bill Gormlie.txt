William Joseph Gormlie (12 May 1911 – 1976) was an English football player and manager. He played as a goalkeeper for Blackburn Rovers and Northampton Town. After his playing career he coached Belgian club R.S.C. Anderlecht and the Belgium national team. He led Anderlecht to the league title five times: in 1951, 1954, 1955, 1956 and 1959. He stood 5 ft 11 in (1.80 m) tall.
