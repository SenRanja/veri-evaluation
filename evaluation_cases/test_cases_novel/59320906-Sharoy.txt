Sharoy (Russian: Шарой, Chechen: Шара, Şara) is a rural locality (a selo) and the administrative center of Sharoysky District, the Chechen Republic, Russia. (The selo of Khimoy serves as the administrative center of Sharoysky Municipal District). Population:   203 (2002 census);
