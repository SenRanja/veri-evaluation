Pentre-rhew is a hamlet in the  community of Llanddewibrefi, Ceredigion, Wales, which is 58.3 miles (93.9 km) from Cardiff and 169.6 miles (272.9 km) from London. Pentre-rhew is represented in the Senedd by Elin Jones (Plaid Cymru) and is part of the Ceredigion Preseli constituency in the House of Commons.


== Etymology ==
The name derives from the Welsh language: "the ice village".
