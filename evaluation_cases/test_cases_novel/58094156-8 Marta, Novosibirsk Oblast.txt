8 Marta (Russian: 8 Марта) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Verkh-Tulinskoye Rural Settlement of Novosibirsky District, Russia. The population was 334 as of 2010.

== Geography ==
8 Marta is located 18 km southwest of Novosibirsk (the district's administrative centre) by road. Krasny Vostok is the nearest rural locality.
