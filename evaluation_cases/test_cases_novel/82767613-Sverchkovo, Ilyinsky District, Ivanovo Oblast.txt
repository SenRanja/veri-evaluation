Sverchkovo (Russian: Сверчково) is a rural locality (a village) in Ilyinsky District, Ivanovo Oblast, Russia. Population: 35 (2010 census); 34 (2002 census); 


== Geography ==
This rural locality is located 16 kilometres (9.9 mi) from Ilyinskoye-Khovanskoye (the district's administrative centre), 66 kilometres (41 mi) from Ivanovo (capital of Ivanovo Oblast) and 185 kilometres (115 mi) from Moscow. Zarubino is the nearest rural locality.
