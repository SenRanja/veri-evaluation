Tekutovo (Russian: Текутово) is a rural locality (a village) in Voskresenskoye Rural Settlement, Cherepovetsky District, Vologda Oblast, Russia. The population was 5 as of 2002.


== Geography ==
Tekutovo is located 72 km northwest of Cherepovets (the district's administrative centre) by road. Privalino is the nearest rural locality.
