The Christine Rose is a self-propelled barge excavator dredge used to mine Bering Sea placer gold deposits in the region around Nome, Alaska, US. It is owned by Pomrenke Mining and its registered home port is Nome, Alaska, US. The Christine Rose is featured in the Discovery Channel USA mining reality TV show Bering Sea Gold.
