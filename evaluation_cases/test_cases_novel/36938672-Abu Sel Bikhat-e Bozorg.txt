Abu Sel Bikhat-e Bozorg (Persian: ابوسلبيخات بزرگ, also Romanized as Abū Sel Bīkhāt-e Bozorg; also known as Abū Salījān, Abū Salīkhān, Abusalixân, Abū Seley Bīkhāt, Abū Seleyb Khān, Boneh, Bonneh, and Kūpāl) is a village in Gheyzaniyeh Rural District, in the Central District of Ahvaz County, Khuzestan Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 140, in 31 families.
